# Feature Requirements (R2 Release)

## Onboarding & Setup
* **R2-1 — Tracking snippet after registration:** Immediately show a copyable, ready-to-use tracking snippet after adding a site.
* **R2-2 — Tracking status:** On Settings/sites, show something simple like *Receiving data / No data yet*, perhaps with the timestamp of the most recent pageview. This would make setup troubleshooting much easier.
* **R2-3 — Tracking snippet available later:** Let you reopen/copy a site's snippet from Settings rather than only seeing it during registration.

## Analytics & Reporting
* **R2-4 — Custom date range:** R1 has Today/7d/30d/90d. Add a simple date picker to select a custom range.
* **R2-5 — Previous-period comparison:** KPI cards could show something like *1,284 visitors ↑ 12%* compared with the immediately preceding equivalent period.
* **R2-6 — Better Overview drill-down:** Clicking a page, referrer, country, browser, etc. could take you to the corresponding report rather than making the Overview panels dead ends.

## Site & Data Management
* **R2-7 — Basic data maintenance:** Since this is your database, a simple way to delete/reset analytics for one site could be useful. This should require deliberate confirmation.
* **R2-8 — Site management polish:** Rename/edit domain/delete site from Settings.
* **R2-9 — Lightweight tracking diagnostics:** For each site: last event received, maybe events received today, and whether geography/technology enrichment is functioning. Useful when you change one of your websites and wonder whether you broke tracking.
* **R2-10 — Active sites overview table:** Table on Settings page with all active sites with basic tracking info, date registered, total hits logged, last hit received.
